About

I started in server rooms. For ten years I ran network infrastructure for Serbia's national pension fund, then built the public site where people view their own pension records.

Since 2019 I've built enterprise platforms at Cognizant Netcentric in Berlin, much of it with the client: requirement workshops, estimates, and presenting to the people who decide budgets.

For the last two years I've designed the multi-agent systems that write production code alongside those teams, connected to Jira, Figma, Confluence and the browser.
