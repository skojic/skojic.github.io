About

I started in server rooms. For ten years I ran network infrastructure for Serbia's national pension fund: 3,000+ workstations, 40+ servers and the disaster recovery plan behind them.

That's also where I started building software. In 2016 the interfaces I saw being made were hard to use, so I built them myself: frontends for internal process tools, and the public site where people across Serbia log in to view their own pension records. By 2018 I'd switched to software full time.

Since 2018 I've also consulted, through Senor, for AutoSync in Oslo, Norway's largest toll-billing provider and a bank-owned financial-services company. I built Kjapp, Senor's parking app, end to end on Node.js and MongoDB, designed seven automation processes that pull national-registry data into invoicing and the CRM, and since 2024 have led the multi-agent orchestration across AutoSync's processes.

Since 2019 I've been at Cognizant Netcentric in Berlin, building enterprise web platforms in JavaScript and TypeScript for Merck, Celonis, Henkel, AbbVie, UBS and BAT. Much of that work sits with the client: requirement workshops, estimates, and presenting solutions to the people who decide budgets.

For the last two years I've designed the multi-agent systems that now write production code alongside those teams, connected to Jira, Figma, Confluence and the browser. On the Merck frontend workstream, delivery time fell 60–70%.
